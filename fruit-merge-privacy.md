Last updated: September 15, 2026

Fruit Merge is an offline puzzle game provided by CoffeeCup Dev. This policy applies to the Android app with package name `com.fabiodev404.fruitmerge`.

### Information collected and shared

Fruit Merge does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, usage analytics, or advertising data to CoffeeCup Dev or third parties. The app does not include advertising, analytics, account registration, social features, or in-app purchases. Its Android release does not request internet access.

### Progress and preferences on your device

The game stores your game progress, in-game coin balance, selected background and music, and audio preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to resume your game and remember your choices. In-game coins have no real-world monetary value.

You can erase this local information through Android's app-storage settings or by uninstalling the app. If you enable Android device backup or transfer, the operating system may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings and Google's policies. CoffeeCup Dev does not operate a cloud-save service or receive these backups.

### Children's privacy

Fruit Merge is intended for players of all ages, including children. The game does not collect personal information from children or other players and does not include ads, chat, or purchases.

### Support

If you choose to email support, we receive the information you include in that message and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information. Parents or guardians should contact support on behalf of children. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

### Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page and the policy available in the app will be updated. The date above identifies the latest update.
